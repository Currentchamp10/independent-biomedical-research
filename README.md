# Independent Research Portfolio in Molecular & Cellular Biomedicine

**Molecular & Cellular Neuroscience | Synaptic Plasticity | Neurodegeneration | Membrane Biology | Ageing | Therapeutic Discovery**

This repository contains a collection of independent literature-based research analyses examining mechanisms across molecular, cellular, synaptic, and systems levels of biomedicine.

## Research Analyses

### 01 — Nanoscale Synaptic Organization and Activity-Dependent Plasticity

Glutamate receptor dynamics, nanoscale organization, CaMKII signalling, receptor trafficking, and structural plasticity.

### 02 — Synaptic Resilience in Alzheimer's Disease

Aβ, tau, synaptic homeostasis, proteostasis, membrane organization, and compensatory mechanisms.

### 03 — Cellular Stress and Signalling in Neural Dysfunction

Neuroinflammation, mTOR signalling, metabolism, autophagy, and synaptic homeostasis.

### 04 — Neural Circuits, Cholinergic Modulation and Ageing

Neuromodulation, neural circuits, memory, cognition, and age-related dysfunction.

### 05 — From Molecular Homeostasis to Cognitive Decline

An integrative analysis connecting molecular, membrane, cellular, synaptic, circuit, and behavioural mechanisms.
