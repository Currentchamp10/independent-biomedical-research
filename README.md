# Independent Research Portfolio in Molecular & Cellular Biomedicine

**Molecular & Cellular Neuroscience | Synaptic Plasticity | Neurodegeneration | Biomembrane Biology | Ageing | Therapeutic Discovery**

This repository presents a collection of independent literature-based research analyses exploring biological mechanisms across molecular, cellular, synaptic, and systems levels of biomedicine.

The portfolio focuses particularly on how molecular and cellular processes influence neuronal function, synaptic plasticity, neurodegeneration, cognitive dysfunction, ageing, and therapeutic development.

## Research Analyses

### 01 — Nanoscale Synaptic Organization and Activity-Dependent Plasticity

An analysis of glutamate receptor dynamics, nanoscale synaptic organization, CaMKII signalling, receptor trafficking, membrane organization, and structural plasticity.

**Focus:** Synaptic plasticity · Glutamate receptors · Membrane organization · CaMKII · Receptor trafficking · Structural plasticity

---

### 02 — Synaptic Resilience in Alzheimer's Disease

An analysis of how synapses respond to Alzheimer’s disease-associated stress, with emphasis on Aβ, tau, synaptic homeostasis, proteostasis, membrane organization, and compensatory mechanisms.

**Focus:** Neurodegeneration · Synaptic dysfunction · Aβ · Tau · Proteostasis · Synaptic resilience · Cognitive dysfunction

---

### 03 — Cellular Stress and Signalling in Neural Dysfunction

An analysis of the interactions among neuroinflammation, cellular signalling, metabolism, autophagy, and synaptic homeostasis in neural dysfunction.

**Focus:** Neuroinflammation · Cellular signalling · mTOR · Metabolism · Autophagy · Synaptic homeostasis

---

### 04 — Neural Circuits, Cholinergic Modulation and Ageing

An analysis of how neuromodulatory systems and neural circuit function contribute to cognition and how these mechanisms change during ageing.

**Focus:** Neural circuits · Cholinergic signalling · Neuromodulation · Memory · Cognition · Ageing

---

### 05 — From Molecular Homeostasis to Cognitive Decline

An integrative analysis connecting molecular and membrane processes with cellular homeostasis, synaptic function, neural circuits, behaviour, ageing, and therapeutic discovery.

**Focus:** Molecular homeostasis · Membrane biology · Synaptic function · Neural circuits · Brain ageing · Therapeutic discovery

## Experimental and Analytical Approaches

The portfolio examines research employing approaches including:

**Microscopy:** confocal microscopy, super-resolution imaging, live-cell imaging, and advanced fluorescence imaging

**Neurophysiology:** electrophysiology, synaptic recordings, neuronal activity analysis, and behavioural neuroscience

**Molecular and cellular methods:** immunofluorescence, Western blotting, PCR, protein-interaction approaches, cellular signalling assays, and related molecular techniques

**Research models:** primary neuronal cultures, rodent models, cellular disease models, and model organisms

**Quantitative approaches:** image analysis, fluorescence quantification, single-particle tracking, computational modelling, and data analysis

## About This Portfolio

The analyses are developed through critical reading and synthesis of peer-reviewed scientific literature, with emphasis on primary research, experimental evidence, methodological interpretation, and unresolved mechanistic questions.

The purpose of the portfolio is to document an evolving scientific interest in the relationships among molecular mechanisms, cellular organization, neuronal function, disease processes, ageing, and therapeutic intervention.
